# 👨🏻‍🎓 Biography

Dr. Wu is currently a Postdoctoral Fellow in Research Centre for Low Altitude Economy, Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, under the supervision of Prof. [Wen-Hua Chen](https://www.engr.uvic.ca/~yshi/index.html).

He is a Youth Editorial Board Member of Journal of Artificial Intelligence & Control Systems, Session Chair. He is also an Organized Session Chair of the 2025 ACIRS. He was selected for the inaugural Doctoral Special Program of Young Elite Scientist Sponsorship Program by China Association for Science and Technology (CAST) in 2025. I obtained twice National Scholarships for Doctoral Students (Top 1% in Shanghai Jiao Tong University).

His research interests include distributed control, safety-critical control, reforcement learning, game-based optimation, and their applications to autonomous vehicle systems.
